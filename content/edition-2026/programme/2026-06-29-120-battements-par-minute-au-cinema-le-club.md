(Re) discover the movie 120 BPM in theaters, on the occasion of the Pride Month, and in its English subtitled version! This session is for English speakers, but not only! Come and chat after the film with Marie from French With Me, for a dialogue between different languages and cultures!

The Movie : Paris, the early 90s. With AIDS already claiming lives for a decade, the activists of Act Up-Paris ramp up their direct-action protests to shatter a wall of public indifference. Amidst the chaos of meetings and demonstrations, newcomer Nathan finds his world upended by Sean—a veteran of the group whose radical passion and defiance change everything.
